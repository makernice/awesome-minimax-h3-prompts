<summary><b>Prompt</b> (1,924 characters)</summary>

```text
Use @Image1 as the strict first frame and primary visual reference. Preserve the model’s face, messy updo with soft bangs, gold hoop earrings, pendant necklace, chocolate-brown draped strapless top with center knot and hanging front panel, ivory wide-leg drawstring pants, flat sandals, body proportions, and the warm minimalist interior with arched niche, cream walls, stone floor, and soft sunlight.

Create a polished 15-second luxury e-commerce fashion video.

0–4s: Begin with the exact full-body front-facing pose from @Image1. The model stands relaxed with both hands in her pockets in a warm sunlit interior. Slow dolly-in from full body to medium full shot. Maintain accurate garment colors, clean shape, and crisp visibility of the top drape and wide-leg pants.

4–7s: Smooth cut to a three-quarter angle, then a natural side profile turn. The model shifts her weight subtly, showing the fit of the bandeau top, the hanging center panel, waistband detail, and the relaxed flow of the pants. Soft natural light creates gentle highlights and shadow definition.

7–10s: Medium full-body tracking shot as the model takes three slow elegant steps forward. The pants move fluidly and independently with realistic fabric physics. She removes one hand from a pocket and lightly adjusts the draped front panel of the top once, then relaxes naturally.

10–13s: Refined close-up sequence of the gathered center knot, soft fabric texture of the top, drawstring waistband, trouser drape, gold jewelry, and sandal detail. Shallow depth of field, soft sunlight, clean texture rendering, stable garment geometry.

13–15s: Return to a full-body hero composition. The model settles into a relaxed fashion pose, looking toward camera with subtle confidence. Premium boutique-commercial finish, elegant motion, stable anatomy, accurate ivory and brown tones, clean lifestyle background, clear apparel visibility, no onscreen text.
2/3
```
