```text
Create a 15-second, 16:9, 24fps high-energy character reveal trailer with exactly 13 distinct cuts.
Style: AAA motion-graphics title sequence × premium Korean fashion campaign × editorial fashion film × explosive character reveal trailer.
Core visual theme: SCARLET RIOT
Visual initials: SR
Tagline: TOO HOT TO STAY STILL.
Character lock:
Use the uploaded woman as the only and absolute identity reference. Preserve the same face, facial proportions, hairstyle silhouette and hair volume, skin tone, body proportions, age impression, and Korean idol/model aura throughout. She is a young adult East Asian woman with a refined small face, fair natural skin, long dark brown to black-brown hair, soft airy bangs, clean features, and a cool yet elegant gaze.
Do not inherit the original light-blue outfit from the reference. The reference is for identity only, not styling.
New wardrobe:
Upgrade her into a red-themed high-fashion Korean idol campaign look: cropped red-and-white plaid blazer, fitted cream or light beige inner top or shirt, deep burgundy tie or ribbon bow, high-waisted black or burgundy mini skirt or tailored shorts, black or burgundy boots, and refined gold accessories. The look should feel sexy, chic, luxurious, vintage-inspired, sharp, and editorial — never vulgar.
Scene:
One consistent setting only: a cream-colored vintage car interior with visible white farmhouse, green field, and golden-hour sunlight outside the window. No random environment changes.
Structure:
The film is 80% graphic motion design / 20% character action. Energy comes mainly from giant kinetic typography, red geometric circles, torn paper textures, red plaid fragments, gold foil stamps, film sprockets, split screens, hard cuts, light leaks, typography slams, poster layouts, editorial grids, sun flares, technical arcs, and halftone textures.
Color system:
Primary colors: bright red, deep burgundy, cherry red, cream white, champagne gold, warm beige, black.
Minor accents: sage green, sky blue.
No pink-dominant or light-blue-dominant palette.
Character action:
No dancing. Use subtle but powerful fashion actions only: direct gaze, slight lean-in, head turns, hair whip, seated posture adjustment, slight forward lean, chin lift, and calm but dominant freezes. Energy: quiet power / calm but dominant / sexy / chic / sharp.
13-cut text system:
Use only these readable English text elements across the trailer:
S / R
SCARLET HOUR
SOFT LIGHT / HARD STARE / SCARLET FEVER
SCARLET
HOT / SHARP / CHIC / WILD
SCARLET RIOT
TOO HOT TO STAY STILL.
Cut flow:
01 pure graphic intro with bold S / R impact.
02 eye close-up framed through a transformed graphic/window shape.
03 giant SR letters with her sliding into frame.
04 split-screen freezes with SCARLET HOUR.
05 rotating text ring: SOFT LIGHT / HARD STARE / SCARLET FEVER.
06 editorial card with giant SCARLET.
07 turn motion with outlined TURN.
08 beat-hit text barrage: HOT / SHARP / CHIC / WILD.
09 black field with farmhouse motif and stamp-like framing.
10 forward lean through shattering graphic panes revealing S → R.
11 rapid poster montage, four layouts, numbered 01–04.
12 hero climax on clean cream background with concentric red shockwave graphics.
13 final identity card with huge SR, then full title SCARLET RIOT and tagline TOO HOT TO STAY STILL.
Editing:
Every cut must feel compositionally different. Use hard cuts, snap zooms, whip pans, stroboscopic freezes, foreground wipes, impact shakes, light-leak splits, and speed ramps. Typography must always be readable before overlap.
Negative constraints:
No face drift, no character replacement, no hairstyle change, no random outfit change, no original blue outfit, no extra people, no 3D cartoon look, no cheap vlog feel, no low-quality CGI, no unreadable text, no messy layout, no watermark, no subtitles, no UI, no logos.
Final feel:
One locked heroine from the uploaded image, transformed into a red luxury Korean editorial icon inside a vintage-car golden-hour world, driven by AAA motion graphics and the SCARLET RIOT visual system.
```
